The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. Because we collect no information, we share none with anyone.

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights and data deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove the progress saved on your device, clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact
